MarketEdge ("we", "us") is operated by [Legal entity name], [Registered address]. This policy explains what information MarketEdge handles when you use it, why, and who it is shared with.

1. What we handle

  • Business information you enter: your business name, the brief you write or dictate, and your selections (industry, stage, target market, goal), plus any questions, revision requests or regeneration notes you type.
  • Generated content: the scope of work, market research, reports, competitor matrices, financial models, business plans and pitch decks produced for you.
  • Technical error reports: when the app hits an error, it sends the error message, the page address and your browser type to our server so we can fix the problem. These reports do not include your brief or reports.

2. Where your data is stored

Your engagements — briefs, generated reports and models — are stored in your own browser (browser local storage). We do not keep a copy of them on our servers. Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, means that content is no longer available, so download anything you want to keep.

3. Third-party AI processing

MarketEdge uses Anthropic's Claude API to generate content. When you generate a scope, research, report, model or deck, the relevant parts of your brief, your selections, and previously generated content for that engagement are sent to Anthropic for processing, and the result is returned to your browser. Anthropic processes this data as our service provider under its commercial terms; see anthropic.com/legal for how Anthropic handles API data.

Market research uses Claude's web search tool: search queries derived from your brief are run on the public web to find sources. Do not put information in your brief that you are not comfortable being processed this way — for example personal data about third parties, or confidential information you are not permitted to share.

5. How we use information

Only to provide the service you request — generating and exporting your analysis — and to keep the service working (fixing errors, preventing abuse). We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

6. Retention

Content in your browser stays until you delete it or clear your browser data. Server logs, including error reports, are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete your data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing; contact us at [Contact email] and we will respond within the time the law requires.

8. Children

MarketEdge is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.
